# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2025  
**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

## Data Collection Statement

**Romper Sample Manager collects NO user data whatsoever.**

This application:
- Does **not** collect any personal information
- Does **not** send your samples, kits, settings or usage to any server
- Does **not** track user behavior or usage patterns
- Does **not** store any identifiable information about users
- Does **not** use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting services

## When Romper Goes Online

Romper works offline. It connects to the internet in only two cases:

- **Factory samples**, when you choose to download them in the setup wizard. Romper downloads Squarp's sample archive from `data.squarp.net`.
- **Update checks, on macOS.** At launch and then weekly, Romper asks `update.electronjs.org` (a free service run by the Electron project) whether a newer release exists, and downloads it if so. The request carries Romper's version, your operating system and its processor type, which the update service needs; like any web request, it also reveals your IP address to that service.

Neither request includes your samples, kits, settings or anything about how you use Romper. Links in the app (the manuals, GitHub) open in your web browser.

## Local Data Processing

Romper operates entirely on your local machine and only processes:
- **Sample files** that you explicitly choose to work with
- **Configuration settings** stored locally on your device
- **Database records** for kit organization, stored locally in SQLite

All data remains on your device and under your complete control.

## Third-Party Services

This application does not send your data to any third-party service, analytics platform, or external API. The only third-party services it contacts are the two above: Squarp's download server and the Electron project's update service.

## Data Security

Since no user data is collected or transmitted, there are no data security concerns related to remote data handling. All sample files and configuration data remain locally on your device.
